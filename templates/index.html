What is JERNIHin?

Safe and accessible water is essential for public health. It is used for drinking, household needs, food production, and recreational activities.

The Water Quality Index (WQI) is an important measurement tool used to determine water quality. It combines several water quality parameters into a single index value.

We use Water Quality Index data to understand drinking water safety and apply machine learning to distinguish between safe and unsafe water.

JERNIHin is a web-based application that predicts water quality and determines whether the water is suitable for consumption.

What is Water Quality Index?

"Save Water, Save Life"

The Water Quality Index (WQI) is a simple method used to evaluate water quality. Water quality can be influenced by natural processes as well as human activities. A higher index value generally indicates better water quality. This system evaluates water quality using eight important parameters: Fecal, pH, Temperature, Phosphorus, Oxygen, Total Sediment, Nitrogen, and Turbidity.

Importance of Water Quality Monitoring

  1. Essential for Human Life

    Water is one of the most basic necessities for human life. Maintaining good water quality is essential for health and daily activities. Physical, chemical, and biological parameters must be monitored according to appropriate standards.

  2. Safe for Consumption

    Water suitable for consumption should meet appropriate physical, chemical, and biological quality requirements. These include parameters such as temperature, color, sedimentation, oxygen, pH, nutrients, bacteria, algae, and other biological factors.
